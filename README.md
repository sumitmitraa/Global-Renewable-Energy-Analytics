# 🌍 Global Renewable Energy Analytics

An interactive **Excel data analysis and dashboard project** focused on exploring global renewable energy data and transforming raw data into meaningful insights.

The project analyzes energy production, consumption, growth, energy sources, countries, and CO₂ reduction across multiple years and presents the findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to perform a detailed analysis of a global energy dataset and identify important patterns and trends related to renewable energy.

The analysis focuses on:

- Energy production
- Energy consumption
- Annual production trends
- Energy production by source
- Energy production by country
- CO₂ reduction
- Average growth rate
- Interactive filtering and exploration

The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to make the data easier to understand and explore.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Aggregation
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains global renewable energy information across different:

- Countries
- Years
- Energy sources
- Production values
- Consumption values
- Growth rates
- CO₂ reduction metrics

The analysis covers the period from **2000 to 2024**.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard provides an overview of global renewable energy through:

### 🔹 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Energy Production
- Total Energy Consumption
- Average Growth Rate
- CO₂ Reduction

### 🔹 Visual Analysis

- Energy Production by Country
- CO₂ Reduction by Energy Source
- Energy Production by Energy Source
- Annual Energy Production Trend

### 🔹 Interactive Filters

Users can explore the dashboard using:

- Country
- Year
- Energy Source

These filters allow different sections of the dashboard to be analyzed dynamically.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Some of the major observations from the analysis include:

- 🌱 Hydro is a major contributor to renewable energy production.
- 📈 Overall energy production shows an upward trend across the analyzed period.
- ⚡ Total energy production remains higher than total consumption.
- ♻️ Hydro shows a significant contribution toward CO₂ reduction.
- 🌍 Energy production varies considerably across countries and energy sources.
